# Evergreen Vision

Build a premium, award-winning corporate website for EVERGREENINDUSTRY, a company specializing in eco-friendly biodegradable and compostable carry bags / sustainable plastic alternatives.

Use https://envisolbioplast.com/ ONLY as a conceptual/content reference. Do NOT copy its UI, layout, styling, visuals, branding, or overall appearance. Create a completely original, significantly more premium experience.

CORE GOAL

The website should immediately communicate:
Nature • Sustainability • Innovation • Quality • Responsible Manufacturing

This is NOT an ecommerce website. Products are for display/showcase only. Do not add cart, checkout, online purchasing, pricing, or ecommerce functionality.

PAGES

Create exactly 7 premium pages, following the same broad information structure/page coverage as the reference website, but redesign everything from scratch:

Home

About Us

Products

Applications / Solutions

Sustainability

Why Choose Us

Contact Us

VISUAL DIRECTION

Create an award-winning environmental brand experience — sophisticated, cinematic, natural and modern.

Use:

Premium botanical green + deep forest green + warm natural beige/cream + white

High-end editorial typography

Large cinematic imagery

Realistic biodegradable carry bags and sustainable packaging photography

Natural textures, leaves, paper, compostable materials and subtle organic elements

Premium whitespace and strong visual hierarchy

Soft glass/organic surfaces where appropriate

Rounded premium cards

Subtle gradients

Elegant shadows

No generic AI-template appearance

No excessive green everywhere

No childish eco icons

No cheap-looking stock imagery

Images must be high-resolution, realistic, premium commercial photography style and visually connected to biodegradable bags, sustainable packaging, manufacturing, nature and eco-friendly materials.

HOME HERO

Create a powerful cinematic hero section with a premium biodegradable carry bag visual integrated with a natural environment.

Headline direction:
“Better Packaging. A Greener Tomorrow.”

Supporting message should communicate that Evergreen Industry creates sustainable alternatives to conventional plastic without compromising usability, durability and business needs.

Add elegant CTA buttons:
Explore Products
Discover Our Story

Hero should have:

Smooth motion

Floating organic particles

Subtle 3D depth

Parallax layers

Premium reveal animations

Slow cinematic image movement

Smooth scrolling

HOME SECTIONS

Create visually impressive sections for:

Brand introduction

Sustainability promise

Featured products

Applications

Why Evergreen Industry

Environmental impact / sustainability metrics

Manufacturing / innovation story

Final CTA

Product showcase should use large premium cards, not basic grid cards.

PRODUCTS

Create a premium product showcase page.

Include products such as:

Biodegradable Carry Bags

Compostable Shopping Bags

Garbage / Waste Bags

Food & Packaging Solutions

Compostable Packaging Products

Other sustainable plastic alternatives

Each product card should include:

Premium product image

Product name

Short description

Application

Material / sustainability highlight

“View Details” interaction

Products are display only.

Use sophisticated hover interactions:
image zoom, depth movement, soft glow, card lift and smooth transitions.

ABOUT US

Tell Evergreen Industry's story around sustainable innovation, responsible manufacturing and replacing conventional plastic with environmentally responsible alternatives.

Use editorial storytelling with large imagery, split layouts and animated statistics.

APPLICATIONS / SOLUTIONS

Show where the products can be used:

Retail

Grocery

Food & Restaurants

Agriculture

Events

Hospitality

Waste Management

Commercial Businesses

Create visually rich application cards with realistic contextual imagery.

SUSTAINABILITY

Make this page visually powerful.

Explain:

Biodegradable materials

Compostable solutions

Reduced plastic dependency

Responsible production

Environmental responsibility

Circular / sustainable thinking

Use an interactive visual storytelling approach instead of boring text blocks.

Include an elegant “from material → product → responsible disposal” visual journey.

WHY CHOOSE US

Create premium trust-building sections around:

Sustainable product solutions

Quality-focused manufacturing

Practical durability

Custom solutions

Responsible business practices

Consistent product quality

Use animated statistics / feature cards where useful.

CONTACT

Premium minimal contact experience.

Include:

Contact form

Company contact details

Location section

Phone/email placeholders

Google Maps placeholder

Strong sustainability-themed CTA

ANIMATION / INTERACTION

Use Framer Motion + GSAP/ScrollTrigger where appropriate.

Important:
Animations must feel luxury and cinematic, not excessive.

Use:

Smooth page transitions

Scroll reveal

Parallax

3D perspective card movement

Image masking reveals

Text stagger animation

Floating organic particles

Magnetic buttons

Premium hover effects

Smooth scrolling

Subtle 3D depth

Section-to-section visual transitions

Hero must create an immediate WOW feeling.

IMPORTANT DESIGN RULES

Do NOT make it look like:

A normal corporate template

A generic AI-generated website

The reference website

A basic green website

An ecommerce store

A simple Bootstrap-style website

Instead make it feel like a high-end sustainability brand / international premium company website.

Prioritize visual impact, typography, spacing, imagery, motion and storytelling over excessive text.

Make the entire experience fully responsive:
desktop + tablet + mobile.

Use reusable components and clean architecture so products/content can easily be updated later.

Brand name everywhere:
EVERGREENINDUSTRY

Final result should feel like a premium award-winning environmental brand website, with the first impression being:
“This is a serious, modern, innovative sustainable-products company.”
